Refund and cancellation policy.

Donations are generally non-refundable. Failed UPI, duplicate debits and clear mistypes are the exceptions. The Tuesday clinic is free.

Effective 13 September 2026. Last updated 13 September 2026. These pages are for this website and for payment-partner onboarding (including PhonePe Business).

1. Scope

This Refund Policy (including cancellation) applies to money sent to Kast Nivaran Balaji Mandir Foundation through this website, UPI, QR code, PhonePe, Google Pay, NEFT/IMPS/RTGS, cash counted at the office, or any other instrument in the Foundation’s name. It is the refund policy URL for payment-partner onboarding, including PhonePe Business.

The Foundation is a Section 8 charity. It is not an e-commerce store. A donation is a gift, not a purchase that can be “cancelled” like an order of goods.

2. General rule — donations are non-refundable

Once a gift has credited the Foundation current account (Union Bank of India, account 524001010060627, IFSC UBIN0552402), it is generally non-refundable. The money is intended for charitable objects: clinic, campus, animals and administration. We do not reverse a gift because you later change your mind, because a project is not yet built, or because you expected a personal benefit.

3. When we will refund or reverse

We will try to refund or help reverse in these cases only:

  1. Failed or pending UPI / PhonePe payment. If your app debited you but the Foundation was not credited, the bank or UPI / PhonePe partner should auto-reverse under NPCI rules. Write to us with the UTR if it has not come back in 7 working days so we can confirm our ledger.
  2. Duplicate payment. The same donor paid the same intended gift twice by obvious error (same amount, same day, two UTRs). Ask within 7 days of the second debit.
  3. Wrong amount clearly mistyped. Example: ₹50,000 sent where ₹5,000 was intended, and you write within 7 days with proof. We may refund the excess after we confirm the credit. We will not refund a gift you simply regret.
  4. Payment to us in error. You meant another payee and can show it, and the credit is still identifiable and unused.
  5. Unauthorised payment. Report to your bank / PhonePe immediately and copy our mailbox. Chargeback and UPI-fraud processes of the payment partner apply.
  6. Legal order. A competent court, tax or payment-partner mandate requires a reversal.

4. When we will not refund

  • Change of mind after a successful credit
  • Dissatisfaction with project speed, clinic wait, or a Tuesday slot being full
  • A gift made to a personal account that is not the Foundation’s — we cannot refund money we never received
  • UPI scan errors where no debit occurred
  • Bank or PhonePe fees, GST on partner charges, or your own mobile data costs
  • 80G not available for your year, or a provisional 12A/80G later not matching your expectation — ask before you give
  • Foreign contribution sent despite this site not inviting it

5. Cancellation

There is no shopping-cart checkout to cancel. A UPI request you have not yet approved can be cancelled in your app. After you enter the PIN and the payee is the Foundation, cancellation is the same as a refund request under section 3. Clinic slots may be cancelled by email; there is no fee because the OPD is free.

6. Clinic

The Dhanvantari clinic does not charge a consultation fee. There is nothing to refund for a missed Tuesday. Medicines given at the OPD are charitable, not sold, and are not refunded in cash.

7. How to ask

Email support@kashtnivaranbalajimandirfoundation.org with subject “Refund request” and include:

  • Full name and phone or email used at payment
  • Amount, date, UTR / UPI / PhonePe transaction ID
  • Account or UPI handle the money should return to (must match the original payer where the partner requires it)
  • Reason, with the matching ground in section 3
  • If an 80G receipt was already issued, say so — it must be cancelled if we refund

We aim to acknowledge within 7 working days. Approved refunds are sent to the original instrument where PhonePe / the bank allows, otherwise by NEFT to the payer’s account, usually within 7–15 working days after approval. We do not pay cash refunds. Payment-partner processing time is extra.

8. PhonePe and other gateways

If you paid through PhonePe Business or a PhonePe payment link, failed transactions follow PhonePe and NPCI reversal rules. Settled donations follow this policy. A refund we approve may be pushed through PhonePe’s merchant dashboard to the original source.

9. Tax receipts

If we refund a gift after a receipt or 80G certificate was issued, that certificate is cancelled. You must not claim a deduction for a refunded amount. 12A and 80G remain provisional as stated on the transparency page.
