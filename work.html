सेवा · Our work

The clinic is running. The campus is being raised.

Seva, samarpan and a healthier neighbourhood — one Tuesday OPD now, five homes of care as land and partners come in.

Open now

Every Tuesday the Dhanvantari Electro Homeopathic Charitable Clinic offers free consultation and treatment at Shivalay Pur, Dallu Kundeshwari. That OPD is the seed: it already serves the public and will later serve school children, elders and shelter residents on the same campus.

Being planned

  • Vidya Mandir — low-fee / sponsored schooling, books, meals, tuition
  • Gaushala — shelter, fodder and veterinary care for abandoned cattle
  • Vriddhashram — residential care for elders without family support
  • Anathashram — family-style home for unsupported children
  • Pashu Seva Kennel — sterilisation, vaccination, adoption, rescue

How to take part

Donate toward land and running costs, volunteer on clinic Tuesdays, or write if you can offer a plot, fodder, school supplies or veterinary time. Use the office mailbox only — this site does not publish personal phone numbers or names.
