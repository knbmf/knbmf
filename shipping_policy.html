Effective 13 September 2026. Last updated 13 September 2026. These pages are for this website and for payment-partner onboarding (including PhonePe Business).

1. Scope

This Shipping Policy is published so that payment partners (including PhonePe Business, which requires a Shipping policy URL) and visitors can see that Kast Nivaran Balaji Mandir Foundation does not ship goods. It sits with the Terms and Conditions, Return Policy and Refund Policy.

2. We do not ship goods

The Foundation is a Section 8 charity. We do not operate an online store. We do not dispatch products by India Post, courier, bus parcel or cash-on-delivery. If a payment-partner form offers the checkbox “My Business does not ship goods”, that statement is true for us; this page is the public URL that explains it.

  • No shipping charges
  • No delivery timelines
  • No tracking numbers issued by us
  • No COD
  • No international shipping

3. How services are delivered instead

3.1 Clinic

Care is in person at Dhanvantari Electro Homeopathic Charitable Clinic, Shivalay Pur, Dallu Kundeshwari, every Tuesday. Medicines from the OPD are handed to the patient at the clinic. They are not posted.

3.2 Donations

A donation is a transfer of money to the Foundation current account. Nothing is shipped in return. A receipt or 80G certificate, when issued, is sent by email to the address you give, or collected from the registered office by arrangement — not as a paid courier product.

3.3 Campus projects

School, gaushala, vriddhashram, anathashram and kennel work happens on site in the Kashipur region when those units are built. They are not mail-order services.

4. Office collection

If the office ever asks you to collect a document (for example a signed receipt), collection is from the registered office at Heritage City, Jaspur Khurd, Kashipur, Udham Singh Nagar, Uttarakhand 244713, by prior email appointment. That is not shipping.

5. Third parties claiming to ship for us

We do not authorise agents to sell or ship goods in the Foundation’s name. Ignore COD parcels that use our name without a matching email from support@kashtnivaranbalajimandirfoundation.org.
