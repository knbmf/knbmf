Privacy Policy.

How the Foundation collects, uses and protects personal data for donations, PhonePe / UPI payments, clinic bookings and this website.

Effective 13 September 2026. Last updated 13 September 2026. These pages are for this website and for payment-partner onboarding (including PhonePe Business).

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how Kast Nivaran Balaji Mandir Foundation (“the Foundation”, “we”) collects, uses, stores and shares personal data when you use this website, donate, pay through UPI / PhonePe / bank transfer, book the Tuesday clinic, volunteer, or email the office. It is meant to meet Indian law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) as it applies, and the expectations of payment partners such as PhonePe Business.

Data fiduciary: Kast Nivaran Balaji Mandir Foundation, CIN U88900UT2025NPL019252, registered office Heritage City, Jaspur Khurd, काशीपुर, ऊधम सिंह नगर, उत्तराखंड 244713. Contact: support@kashtnivaranbalajimandirfoundation.org.

2. What we collect

2.1 You give us

  • Name, email, phone number, postal address
  • PAN, donation amount, date, UTR / UPI reference / PhonePe transaction ID
  • Clinic booking notes (day requested, general reason for visit)
  • Volunteer or press messages
  • Any file or screenshot you attach

2.2 Collected automatically

  • IP address, browser type, device type, referring page
  • Basic server or hosting logs (GitHub Pages / DNS / email host)
  • Cookies or local storage if a donate form or analytics is later enabled

2.3 From payment partners

Union Bank of India, NPCI UPI, PhonePe, Google Pay and similar apps may send us settlement reports: amount, status, VPA handle, transaction reference and time. We do not receive your full card number or UPI PIN.

2.4 What we do not seek

This website does not ask for Aadhaar, passwords, or UPI PIN. Do not send those. Clinic care may involve health information in person; keep email descriptions minimal.

3. Why we use data

  • To receive, match and acknowledge donations
  • To issue receipts and, where eligible, 80G certificates
  • To book and run the charitable clinic
  • To answer support mail and keep a donor / volunteer ledger
  • To meet Companies Act, Income-tax, GST, FCRA (if ever applicable), accounting and audit duties
  • To detect fraud, duplicate payments and misuse of our QR or name
  • To operate and secure the website and payment links

We do not sell personal data. We do not use donor lists for third-party advertising.

4. Legal basis

We process data because you consent (forms and emails), because it is needed to accept a gift or book a slot, and because Indian tax and company law require us to keep books and issue receipts. You may withdraw consent for optional mailing; we still keep records that the law requires.

5. Sharing

We share personal data only as needed with:

  • Union Bank of India and other banks that hold Foundation accounts
  • PhonePe, UPI apps and payment aggregators that process your payment
  • Income Tax Department, GST authorities, MCA, auditors, and professional accountants or lawyers bound to confidentiality
  • Hosting, DNS and email providers that run this domain
  • Courts or police when Indian law requires it

Staff and volunteers see only what they need. This website does not publish donor, patient or director names.

6. Cookies and similar technology

The public pages are static. They do not currently set advertising cookies. A donate or admin tool on a Foundation server may use a session cookie to keep you logged in or to remember a form. Browsers can block cookies; donation and login tools may then fail. We do not run third-party ad networks on this site.

7. Retention

  • Donation, receipt and 80G records: at least eight years, or longer if tax or company law requires
  • Clinic appointment mail: as long as needed for care continuity and legal defence, then deleted or archived
  • Server logs: typically a short hosting window unless needed for security
  • Unsuccessful enquiries: usually up to 24 months

8. Storage and security

Data is stored in India or in the country of a processor (for example a bank or PhonePe). We use password-protected mail, limited admin access, and ordinary hosting HTTPS. No method is perfectly secure. Do not send PAN or health detail on a public social-media comment.

9. Your rights

Subject to the DPDP Act and tax retention rules, you may ask the Foundation to:

  • Confirm whether we hold your data and receive a copy
  • Correct inaccurate name, address, PAN or contact detail
  • Erase data we are not required to keep
  • Withdraw consent for optional messages
  • Nominate a person to be contacted if you cannot exercise rights yourself, where the law allows

Write to support@kashtnivaranbalajimandirfoundation.org with the subject “Privacy request”. We may need to verify it is you. We will not delete a donation record needed for 80G or audit only because a donor later asks.

10. Children

We do not knowingly collect data from children to take donations. A parent or guardian must donate and must book a child’s clinic slot. Anathashram and school projects, when they open, will have additional safeguarding rules.

11. International visitors

The site is aimed at people in India. If you write from abroad, your data is still processed for an Indian Section 8 company. Foreign contribution is not invited on this website.

12. Grievance

Privacy and data grievances: the Foundation office, same registered address, support@kashtnivaranbalajimandirfoundation.org. We aim to acknowledge within 7 working days and resolve within 30 days where the law allows. You may also use any Data Protection Board or consumer forum process that Indian law provides.

This website does not name a grievance officer personally. Use the office mailbox and CIN U88900UT2025NPL019252.

13. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy. The date at the top of the page is the latest version. Material changes to payment or donor data use will also be reflected in the Terms and Conditions.
