Projected

Vidya Mandir

School

A low-fee / sponsored school for children from economically weaker families — books, meals and tuition support as written in the memorandum.

  • Primary and middle classes first
  • Scholarships and school-supply drives
  • After-school tuition for first-generation learners
Projected

Gaushala

Cow shelter

A sheltered gaushala for abandoned, injured and ageing cattle — fodder, shade, veterinary care and a respectful life after they can no longer work.

  • Rescue and treatment of injured cattle
  • Fodder bank and clean water troughs
  • Volunteer seva days for local families
Projected

Vriddhashram

Old-age home

A residential home for elders without family support — meals, a nurse’s round, prayer space and company, not a locked ward.

  • Shared rooms with dignity and privacy
  • Daily meals, medicines and clinic link
  • Festival days open to the neighbourhood
Projected

Anathashram

Children’s home

A non-institutional, family-style home for orphaned and unsupported children — school, food, counselling and a path into skill or higher study.

  • Safe residential care with house parents
  • Tied to the Foundation school and clinic
  • Legal aid and child-rights referral as in the MOA
Projected

Pashu Seva Kennel

Animal shelter

A kennel and first-aid yard for stray and abandoned dogs — sterilisation camps, vaccination, adoption and kind holding, not a pound.

  • ABC / ARV camps with local vets
  • Adoption desk for Kashipur families
  • Emergency rescue for injured animals
