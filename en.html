Who we are

A not-for-profit company limited by shares under Section 8 of the Companies Act, registered at Heritage City, Jaspur Khurd, Kashipur, Udham Singh Nagar, Uttarakhand 244713. The first operating unit is Dhanvantari Electro Homeopathic Charitable Clinic, Shivalay Pur, Dallu Kundeshwari — free consultation and treatment every Tuesday. Projected campus work includes Balaji temple construction, a charitable hospital, school, gaushala, old-age home, children’s home and animal kennel.
