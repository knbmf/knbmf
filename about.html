परिचय · About

A Section 8 charity for Kashipur.

Incorporated on 20 May 2025 as a not-for-profit company limited by shares. Surplus stays in the work. No dividend is paid to members.

Why we exist

The Foundation is registered to do social-work activities without running a commercial hospital or a public share offer. The first working unit is the Dhanvantari charitable clinic. Around it we are raising a campus: a school, a gaushala, an old-age home, a children’s home, and a kennel for abandoned animals.

What the memorandum allows

The objects include community support for children, women, elders, differently-abled people and economically weaker families; health and nutrition; education and scholarships; skill and livelihood training; counselling and legal-aid referral; civic and environmental work; and non-residential rehabilitation. The campus projects sit inside those objects.

Where

Registered office: Heritage City, Jaspur Khurd, Kashipur, Udham Singh Nagar, Uttarakhand 244713. Clinic: Shivalay Pur, Dallu Kundeshwari. The objects extend to the whole of India.

How we are governed

The company is private in the Companies Act sense: shares are not offered to the public, and income cannot be taken out as profit. This website does not list directors or staff by name. For official filings, use the CIN on the transparency page and the MCA public register.
