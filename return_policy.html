Effective 13 September 2026. Last updated 13 September 2026. These pages are for this website and for payment-partner onboarding (including PhonePe Business).

1. Scope

This Return Policy is published so that payment partners (including PhonePe Business, which requires a Return policy URL) and visitors can see how returns work for Kast Nivaran Balaji Mandir Foundation. It sits with the Terms and Conditions, Refund Policy and Shipping Policy.

2. We do not sell goods

The Foundation is a Section 8 charitable company (CIN U88900UT2025NPL019252). This website has no product catalogue, no shopping cart, no SKU, and no retail counter. We do not sell clothes, idols, medicines, books, livestock or campus “packages” online or by courier.

Because there is no sale of goods, there is nothing to return in the consumer-shop sense. If a payment-partner form offers the checkbox “My Business does not support returns”, that statement is true for us; this page is the public URL that explains it.

3. Donations are not products

A donation is a voluntary gift. It cannot be “returned” like an item. Money questions are handled only under the Refund Policy (failed UPI, duplicate debit, clear mistype, unauthorised payment, or legal order).

4. Clinic items

Medicines and consumables given at Dhanvantari Electro Homeopathic Charitable Clinic are part of free Tuesday care. They are not sold, have no MRP invoice as a shop sale, and cannot be returned for cash or replacement as if they were retail goods. If a dispensed item looks damaged or wrongly packed before you leave the clinic, tell the volunteer on duty that day so it can be replaced from clinic stock where appropriate.

5. What to do if something physical reaches you by mistake

We do not run a dispatch desk. If a courier parcel claiming to be from the Foundation arrives and you did not arrange a documented hand-delivery with the office, do not pay COD. Email support@kashtnivaranbalajimandirfoundation.org with photographs. We will say whether it is ours. Unsolicited parcels in our name should be treated as suspicious.

6. Digital receipts

Donation receipts and 80G certificates are records, not goods. They are not “returned”. Errors in name, PAN or amount are corrected by a revised certificate, or cancelled if the gift itself is refunded.
